How to Play

Welcome to Dragon Tamagotchi! The objective of this game is to keep your dragon happy and healthy for as long as possible. Your dragon has four health stats: hunger, cleanliness, happiness, and exercise. It is your responsibility to take the following actions when appropriate: click 'Feed' to increase your dragon's hunger statistic. Click 'Clean' to clean your dragon's cave and increase the cleanliness stat. Click 'Play' to increase your dragon's exercise stat. Finally, click 'Pet' to increase your dragon's happiness stat. Have fun and keep your dragon healthy!
